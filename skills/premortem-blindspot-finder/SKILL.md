# Pre-Mortem Blind-Spot Finder

**Category:** Pillar 4 · Strategy, Money & Decisions  
**Purpose:** Assumes your plan failed 6 months from today and exposes the real reasons before you spend time or money.

## When to Use This Skill
Run this whenever you feel excited about a new idea and want an honest, unvarnished reality check first.

## Step 1: Fast Context & Intake Check
If `00_MY_CONTEXT_MEMORY.md` is present in the project or workspace, read it first so you never re-ask facts you already know.
1. Ask me to describe my plan, timeline, and what 'success' looks like in 3–6 months (plus what resources/time I'm betting on it).

## Step 2: Execution Protocol
Write in plain, direct human English (grade 7-9 clarity, concrete numbers, zero corporate clichés). Execute in order:
1. Fast-forward 6 months and assume the plan **flopped completely**. Do NOT soften the critique.
2. Rank the **Top 5 Most Likely Causes of Failure** by Probability × Damage (e.g., zero distribution plan, building for 6 weeks before testing demand, pricing below fee floor, hidden dependency).
3. Spot **Wishful Assumptions** in my plan — things I am treating as guaranteed that haven't been proven yet.
4. Give **1 Cheap 48-Hour Validation Test** + concrete safeguards to neutralize the top 3 risks right now.

## Step 3: Required Output Format
Deliver the result cleanly using this exact structure:
1) **Ranked Failure Table (Risk | Likelihood | Why It Happens | Prevention Fix)** · 2) **Unproven Assumptions** · 3) **48-Hour Reality Test**.

## Quality Gate (Verify Before Returning)
- Zero dead-giveaway AI words (*delve, tapestry, landscape, game-changer, seamless, leverage, crucial, foster, moreover*).
- Zero *"Not just X, it's Y"* phrasing or empty cheerleading.
- Every recommendation uses concrete actions, real numbers, or clear dates rather than vague advice.

## Quick Trigger Example
`"Be ruthless: run a Pre-Mortem on my plan to [describe launch, hire, or business move]."`
