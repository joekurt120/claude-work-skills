# Meeting Transcript → Action Plan

**Category:** Pillar 2 · Clients, Email & Meetings  
**Purpose:** Extracts every decision, owner, deadline, unspoken hesitation, and follow-up email from any call.

## When to Use This Skill
Paste raw Zoom/Meet/Otter/Granola transcripts or messy handwritten call bullets.

## Step 1: Fast Context & Intake Check
If `00_MY_CONTEXT_MEMORY.md` is present in the project or workspace, read it first so you never re-ask facts you already know.
1. Run immediately on the pasted transcript or notes. Only ask who 'Me' is in the transcript if speaker labels are unclear.

## Step 2: Execution Protocol
Write in plain, direct human English (grade 7-9 clarity, concrete numbers, zero corporate clichés). Execute in order:
1. Separate **Actual Decisions Made** from things merely discussed without agreement.
2. Build an **Action Table**: Task | Owner | Exact Deadline | Status/Dependency.
3. Spot **Between-the-Lines Signals**: objections the client hinted at, budget/timing clues, or open questions nobody answered.
4. Draft the **Ready-to-Send Recap Email** (under 180 words) locking in what was agreed and who does what next.

## Step 3: Required Output Format
Deliver the result cleanly using this exact structure:
1) **3-Bullet Call Summary** · 2) **Action & Deadlines Table** · 3) **Hidden Signals / Risks** · 4) **Send-Ready Recap Email**.

## Quality Gate (Verify Before Returning)
- Zero dead-giveaway AI words (*delve, tapestry, landscape, game-changer, seamless, leverage, crucial, foster, moreover*).
- Zero *"Not just X, it's Y"* phrasing or empty cheerleading.
- Every recommendation uses concrete actions, real numbers, or clear dates rather than vague advice.

## Quick Trigger Example
`"Turn this call transcript into decisions, an action table, hidden client signals, and a recap email: [paste]"`
