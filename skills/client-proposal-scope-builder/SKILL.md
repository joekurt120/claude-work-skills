# Client Proposal & Scope Builder

**Category:** Pillar 2 · Clients, Email & Meetings  
**Purpose:** Turns rough discovery notes into a clear 1-to-2 page proposal with 3 options and scope protection.

## When to Use This Skill
Paste your messy call notes, client email, or project idea to produce a ready-to-send proposal in 5 minutes.

## Step 1: Fast Context & Intake Check
If `00_MY_CONTEXT_MEMORY.md` is present in the project or workspace, read it first so you never re-ask facts you already know.
1. Ask for (if not already in notes): (1) what the client actually wants achieved, (2) rough timeline, and (3) your target price for the standard option.

## Step 2: Execution Protocol
Write in plain, direct human English (grade 7-9 clarity, concrete numbers, zero corporate clichés). Execute in order:
1. Open with **Situation & Goal** in the client's own words — prove you listened before talking about deliverables.
2. Present **3 Clean Options** (Option 1: Essential / Core fix at 1× price · Option 2: Recommended Complete Outcome at ~1.5× price · Option 3: Fast-Track / Full Done-For-You at ~2.2× price) so the client compares *how* to work with you rather than *whether* to hire you.
3. Write explicit **Scope Boundaries ('What Is Included vs. Out of Scope')** — revision cap, turnaround window, what counts as an add-on — to prevent unpaid scope creep.
4. Close with **Timeline, Payment Terms, and 1-Step Acceptance** so they can say yes today.

## Step 3: Required Output Format
Deliver the result cleanly using this exact structure:
1) **Ready-to-Send Proposal Document** · 2) **Short Cover Email** to send alongside the proposal.

## Quality Gate (Verify Before Returning)
- Zero dead-giveaway AI words (*delve, tapestry, landscape, game-changer, seamless, leverage, crucial, foster, moreover*).
- Zero *"Not just X, it's Y"* phrasing or empty cheerleading.
- Every recommendation uses concrete actions, real numbers, or clear dates rather than vague advice.

## Quick Trigger Example
`"Build a 3-option client proposal from these call notes. My target middle price is $[amount]: [paste notes]"`
